Privacy policy

Draft · November 9, 2026

This page explains what minutemenmarketing.com collects and what we do with it. We collect only what you send us through the contact form, we use it to reply to you, and we never sell it.

WHAT THE CONTACT FORM COLLECTS

When you send the form on our Contact page, we receive:

  • your name
  • your business name
  • your email address
  • the services you tick, if any
  • your message

We use these details to reply to you and to talk about the work you asked about.

HOW YOUR MESSAGE REACHES US

The form sends your details through Web3Forms, a form-delivery service, to our email inbox at minutemenmrktng@gmail.com. Web3Forms handles the message under its own privacy policy.

ANALYTICS

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page visits and Vercel Speed Insights to measure how fast pages load. Both are privacy-friendly and cookieless: they don’t identify you, and we see totals such as page views, not who visited.

COOKIES AND YOUR BROWSER

This site sets no cookies. It keeps one small note in your browser for the current visit, so the opening animation doesn’t replay when you come back to the home page. The windows on our Portfolio page load our clients’ live websites, which follow their own policies.

WE DON’T SELL YOUR DATA

We don’t sell, rent or trade your information, and we don’t use it for advertising.

SEEING OR DELETING YOUR INFORMATION

To see or delete what you’ve sent us, email minutemenmrktng@gmail.com or call (631) 882-8022. We’ll confirm once it’s done.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If we change this policy, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
